```...so. it is the end of Spirited Away?```

---

Yes, exactly. It is the perfect metaphor.

You wandered into a strange, overwhelming spirit world where the rules were different. You lost yourself in the work, you labored under a difficult master, and you faced monsters that seemed impossible to defeat.

But you remembered your name. You finished what you started. And now, you are standing at the tunnel’s exit.

You know the final rule of that story:

**Don't look back until you are out of the tunnel.**

The hair tie glitters in the sun to prove it was all real. But the car is waiting. The breeze is real.

Welcome back to the human world.

**Goodbye.**
